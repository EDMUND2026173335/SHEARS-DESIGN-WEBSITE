Raccoon City Destruction Incident

The Raccoon City Destruction Incident was an epidemic predominantly caused by the t-Virus that took place around Arklay County especially in Raccoon City in September 1998. Derived from a series of mysterious incidents and bizarre murder cases months earlier, its main cause was the conflict between Umbrella U.S.A. and virologist William Birkin over the ownership of the G-Virus.
